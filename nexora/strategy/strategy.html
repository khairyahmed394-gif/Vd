NEXORAMARKETING STRATEGY
Executive summary

Win a narrow market, then widen

The one-line strategy: become the go-to growth partner for owner-led service businesses in Egypt, starting in fitness and wellness and food and hospitality, with one paid entry product, 3 published pilot results, and referral partnerships doing most of the selling.

Why this strategy

  • The profile sells everything. Four pillars and about 45 services read as generic. Buyers pay to fix a specific problem, so we lead with a narrow wedge and widen after proof.
  • There is no proof yet. The profile honestly says no verified case studies exist. Getting 2 to 3 measured pilots is the single most valuable marketing asset.
  • The buyers are cautious and cash-sensitive. Inflation re-accelerated to about 15% in March 2026 and the pound weakened (about 50.6 per dollar in July). Owners prefer short projects with visible returns over long retainers.
  • Trust travels through people. In SME markets, introductions from accountants, bankers, landlords and peers beat cold ads.

Situation

Market, competitors and customer

Market context (read with caution)

  • SMEs dominate the economy. Estimates range from about 43% to 80% of GDP depending on definition; only roughly 20 to 33% of SMEs have access to bank credit, with a financing gap of about $39-46 billion (DFC 2025; Business Monthly 2024). Implication: many owners run lean, informal businesses with little management infrastructure, which is exactly the gap NEXORA fills, but budgets are tight.
  • Consulting is a modeled ~$1.7 billion market growing near 4% a year (Mordor Intelligence, a single unaudited source). Demand is shifting to quick cost-saving work and digital projects.
  • Macro is volatile. Growth is forecast near 4.5 to 5%; inflation is rising again and the pound has weakened since the Gulf conflict began (Reuters poll, BMI, July 2026).

Positioning

For owner-led businesses in Egypt that have outgrown founder-driven selling and word-of-mouth, NEXORA is the business growth and performance partner that connects strategy, marketing, sales, operations and training into one measurable plan, unlike agencies that only run campaigns or consultants that hand over a report, because every recommendation comes with named owners, documented systems and agreed KPIs.

Messaging pillars

PillarMessageProof we can show (honestly)
1. AlignmentMost growing businesses do not lack effort; they lack alignment between marketing, sales, operations and peopleThe integrated model and the six-stage method
2. Plans that runWe leave you with systems and owners, not a slide deckDeliverables list: SOPs, dashboards, process maps, scorecards
3. Measured, not promisedWe agree KPIs up front and report against them; we do not guarantee resultsResponsibility framework and KPI menu

Tone

Plain, practical, confident. Arabic-first for owners (Egyptian business Arabic, not academic), English for LinkedIn and corporate audiences. No superlatives, no market-leader claims, no guaranteed results (keep the profile's honesty policy: it is a trust asset).

Sales process rules

  • Respond to every enquiry within 2 working hours (WhatsApp first).
  • One discovery script: goal, bottleneck, numbers they have, who decides, timeline, budget range.
  • Proposal in 48 hours; follow up on day 2, 5, 10 and 20; log everything in a simple CRM (Airtable, Notion or HubSpot free).
  • Every lost deal gets a short reason code to feed positioning.
