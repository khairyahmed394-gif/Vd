## 1. Positioning

**Who:** ClinStat Research, a clinical research and biostatistics consultancy focused on the Gulf / Middle East.
**Tone (from brand.json):** authoritative, calm, educational, premium, plain-English, no hype.
**Tagline in use:** "Data Integrity – Accelerated Trials".

**Services to advertise [CONFIRM the full list and any pricing]:**
1. Pre-submission statistical and ethics review for manuscripts
2. Biostatistics and clinical trial data analysis
3. Survival / time-to-event analysis
4. Meta-analysis and reliability of findings
5. Regional (Gulf) medical insights

**Core message:** *Most research problems that get papers rejected are avoidable with one expert review before submission.*

**Do not claim:** guaranteed acceptance, guaranteed publication, "FDA/regulatory approved", client names, or numbers you cannot document. Use only what ClinStat can prove.

## 4. Budget (50,000 EGP **[CONFIRM: per month, or total?]**)

50,000 EGP is roughly USD 1,000 at recent rates **[CONFIRM current rate and that ads are billed in EGP or USD]**. That is a small budget for four platforms, so spreading it thin would leave every platform below the data needed to optimise. Recommendation: **concentrate on LinkedIn and Meta, and use X organically** (post the same content unpaid). Add paid X later if the first two work.

| Platform | EGP / month | Approx. share | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 20,000 | 40% | One campaign: academics and clinician-researchers, GCC-wide; Lead Gen Forms; 1 to 2 ads |
| Facebook + Instagram | 22,000 | 44% | One campaign: broad country targeting, 3 to 4 ads; Reels reach + instant forms |
| Retargeting (both platforms) | 4,000 | 8% | Video viewers and site visitors; free-consult offer |
| Testing reserve | 4,000 | 8% | Move to the winner after week 2, or trial paid X |
| X (paid) | 0 | n/a | Organic only for now |
| Sponsors (segment C) | 0 paid | n/a | Cold email / named-account outreach outside the ad budget |

**Country split (a reporting target, not separate budgets; starting guess, no data yet):** Saudi Arabia 45%, UAE 35%, Qatar 20%. Qatar's audience is small, so it will saturate quickly; keep it capped. Rebalance by cost per qualified lead after week 2.

**Language by market (starting guess):** Saudi Arabia: Arabic first, English second. UAE: English first, Arabic second. Qatar: Arabic and English in equal measure. Test this rather than assuming it.

Notes:
- LinkedIn clicks cost much more than Meta's, so expect fewer clicks there but higher-quality leads. Check LinkedIn's current minimum daily budget when setting up.
- **Ad-count ceiling:** active ads per campaign should not exceed (daily budget × 14) / (2 × target cost per qualified lead), so each ad can get a fair read in 14 days. At about USD 14.7/day per platform and placeholder targets of USD 100 (LinkedIn) and USD 25 (Meta), that is about 1 and about 4 ads. Recalculate with the real target.
- **Expected volume:** using the playbooks' LinkedIn ranges (CPC USD 8 to 22, Lead Gen Form CPL USD 50 to 200; US B2B SaaS benchmarks, so treat as rough), the LinkedIn budget buys roughly 2 to 9 leads a month. Treat month 1 as a learning month; success is a measured cost per qualified lead, not a lead count.
- **Target cost per qualified lead (TCPL):** breakeven CPL = average project value × consultation-to-client rate, and the target sits below it by the required margin. Set it before launch **[CONFIRM project price, close rate and margin]**.
- **Kill rules:** pause a new ad once it has spent 2 to 3× TCPL with no qualified lead. Pause an ad older than 7 to 14 days when its cost per qualified lead runs 1.5 to 2× over TCPL. Never pause a producing ad without a replacement ready.
- Change budgets in small steps (about 20%) every 3 to 4 days.
- Set a weekly cap, and keep a tracked cost per qualified lead from week 1.

## 8. Tracking and measurement
- **LinkedIn:** Insight Tag + conversion for "consultation booked"; use Lead Gen Forms.
- **Meta:** Pixel + Conversions API; events: ViewContent, Lead, Schedule. Use instant forms for the lead test.
- **X:** Pixel/conversion tracking for form submit.
- UTM format: `utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=clinstat_2026q4&utm_content={ad_name}`
- Log leads in one sheet or CRM with source. Score leads weekly (qualified vs not).
- **Lead quality score:** whoever runs the consultation scores each lead on Urgency, Budget and Fit (0 to 3 each, max 9) and logs it against the ad. After about 20 scored calls, rank ads by average quality score rather than CPL or CTR.
- **Qualifying question:** add one to every lead form (study stage or manuscript status).
- **Feed quality back:** send "consultation booked" (and later "qualified") to Meta Conversions API and the LinkedIn conversion API. Reconcile platform numbers against your own lead sheet monthly; the sheet wins.

**KPIs (set targets once you have a budget and baseline [CONFIRM]):** CTR, cost per qualified lead, consultations booked, video 50%+ views. Do not copy benchmark numbers from the internet without matching your industry, region and objective.

## 9. Compliance and brand safety checklist
- **Meta:** health-related ads can't imply knowledge of a person's medical conditions; avoid before/after claims. Lead forms need a privacy policy link.
- **LinkedIn:** no misleading claims; disclose accurately.
- **X:** read the health/medical advertising policy before launch.
- **Data privacy:** collecting health-research data or personal data in the Gulf (e.g. Saudi PDPL, UAE PDPL) needs a compliant privacy notice. Do not collect patient data through ads or forms.
- Keep claims factual; no guarantees of publication.
- Verify each platform's current policies before launch, as they change.
