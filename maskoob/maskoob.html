Business. مسكوب (MASKOOB Café) is a new café in 10th of Ramadan City, Sharqia. Everything below that concerns the business itself comes from the marketer's own briefing in this session Marketer-reported. Searches on 26 Oct 2026 found no public footprint for the name (no Instagram, Facebook, Maps listing or website), which is consistent with a business that has not opened.

What this means. There is no live brand to audit and no public customer data. The work is therefore a pre-launch engagement: confirm the identity, finish the visual system while the premises are still undesigned, then run a short, measurable launch.

Preliminary opportunity. The city is a very large industrial employment base (Public report Al-Ahram, Sept 2025: about 6,000 companies and 400,000 workers, as stated by the head of the investors association). Public directory listings show only a few organised cafés. That does not prove an unmet need. It is a hypothesis that a distinctive specialty café with a strong identity can own a clear position, to be tested by field research in the first two weeks.

Biggest decision now. The premises are contracted but undesigned, so the identity can still shape the interior brief. That is the highest-leverage and time-limited part of the engagement.

Not claimed anywhere in this document: revenue forecasts, guaranteed results, competitor performance, or owner approval of any brand direction.

Field research protocol (week 1)

  1. On Google Maps, search "كافيه" and "قهوة مختصة" within 5 km of the premises. Record rating, review count, review themes, photos, hours.
  2. On Instagram, search city hashtags and tagged locations. Record posting frequency and format mix. Do not treat followers as sales.
  3. Visit the shortlisted cafés at two day parts. Note price of a flat white or cappuccino and a dessert, seating, crowd and Wi-Fi.
  4. Include non-café substitutes: mall food courts, bakeries, delivery chains, factory canteens.

Market context

Tagline options

  • قهوة بطابعها (working suggestion, supplied). Short and ties to the "character" idea. Check that "بطابعها" reads naturally to local customers.
  • اتسكبت على مهلها: plays on the name; slower, relaxed tone.
  • مسكوب… وبس: playful, colloquial, short.

Options two and three are drafts for owner and customer testing, not approved lines. Have a native copywriter review them.

When the Mediterranean direction may not fit

  • If the premises cannot support terrazzo, arches or generous seating, the identity must still work on flat colour and simple materials.
  • If the local customer expects a lively, sociable, lower-price venue, a calm minimal look may feel formal. Test with the interviews.
  • If specialty coffee is not yet understood locally, the brand needs education content, not just aesthetics.

Logo system

  • Primary: Arabic wordmark with tagline lockup.
  • Compact: wordmark alone for cups, avatars, stamps.
  • One-colour and reversed: Deep Blue on light, Chalk White on Deep Blue.
  • Clear space: equal to the height of the wordmark's tall letter on every side. Minimum size to be set after the vector rebuild [NEED].
  • Misuse: no stretching, no pattern behind the wordmark, no low-contrast colours, no effects.

Graphic language and photography

  • Pour line: single thin line, used as divider or link between sections.
  • Terrazzo pattern: border or panel only; keep text on flat colour.
  • Type grid: margins based on the wordmark's proportions.
  • Photography: soft natural side light, warm neutral grading, hands and process shots, wide interiors in daylight. Real MASKOOB photographs for final use; concept images stay labeled as visualizations. Get written consent from anyone identifiable.

Applications (concepts only, none approved)

Signage, brand wall, cups and sleeves, bags, menu, aprons, loyalty card, social posts, ads. Concept mockups appear on the supplied board. Final artwork needs the rebuilt logo, confirmed prices and real photography.

Conversion path

Discover on Instagram, Facebook or Maps → see menu and photos → check location, hours and phone → visit or order takeaway → leave a review. Needs accurate address, a clear menu link, Maps pin, a phone or WhatsApp number [NEED]. Do not assume delivery, reservations or online ordering exist.

Retention

Paper or digital loyalty card, a "bring a colleague" mechanism, seasonal drinks, a review request after the first visit. Collect contact details only with a stated purpose and consent; check the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020) with legal counsel before building any customer database.
